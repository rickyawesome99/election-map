TRUE PARTISAN LEAN · COUNTIES
The same lean, county by county

County TPL runs the state recipe on each county's share of the same statewide races: president, Senate and governor, plus the county's House vote. Because the code is shared, a change to the state model changes every county with it. It appears on each county page and does not feed the forecast directly.

HOW IT DIFFERS
What changes at county level
Statewide races
The same race in every county, so the incumbent and the money are the state race's. Only the margin is the county's own.
House races
A county often sits in more than one district, so its House vote mixes several races. No single incumbent or money gap can be taken out, and a year where one party ran no one in the county is filled in from its presidential vote.
Outliers
A county far from its state's lean is not an outlier; it's just a different place. Only a House year that runs far from the county's own statewide results is turned down, since a popular local member can carry a county their party usually loses.
Missing places
Alaska reports no county results, so it has no county leans.
