HOW IT DIFFERS
Same recipe, two ingredients

District TPL follows the same steps as the state version, so a district and its state can be compared directly. Two things are different.

1
Only presidential and House races
Senate, governor and state legislative results are not reported by congressional district, so a district's lean comes from the 2016, 2020 and 2024 presidential votes counted inside its 2026 lines, plus its House races since 2016.
2
Old races are moved onto the new lines
When a district has been redrawn, its old House results were cast on different ground. We can't rebuild an old race on new lines, so we shift it by how much more Republican or Democratic the new lines voted for president than the old ones. Races that moved a long way count for less.
Does it use the state's swing?
Yes. A district moves with the national mood as much as its state does.
Does a member's own record affect it?
A little: a long-serving member's own House results are part of their district's lean, the way any past result is.
